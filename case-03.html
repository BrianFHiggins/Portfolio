Overview

Operators using the AMCS Platform had no dedicated place to manage their subcontractors. Vendor relationships, compliance documents, job oversight, and billing all existed in separate places or not at all. The Vendor Management Workcenter brought all of it into one place.

This wasn't a redesign. It was a net new product built within the constraints of the platform design system, following established UX principles to ensure consistency across the many workcenters operators already work in daily.

The Users

Three distinct roles use this workcenter for different purposes.

Vendor Managers
Dashboard & Compliance
Use the dashboard daily to monitor performance across all subcontractors, spot compliance issues before they block new work, and get a quick read on which vendors are reliable.
Operations Managers
Jobs & Oversight
Live on the jobs screen, tracking what's been accepted, rejected, and completed across their vendor network throughout the day.
Accountants
Billing & Transactions
Work in the billing section, pulling together completed job transactions and matching them into bills for specific vendors rather than dealing with one unstructured lump sum.
The Product
Dashboard

The entry point gives an immediate read on vendor health. Performance bars show each vendor's completed versus rejected job ratio. A rating score is calculated automatically based on job acceptance, completion and rejection rates combined with compliance status. Operators told us this was immediately useful for deciding who to trust with new work.

Vendors

A table of all vendors with compliance status and ongoing job counts at a glance. Colour-coded compliance badges make non-compliant and expiring vendors immediately visible without drilling in.

Clicking a vendor opens their profile. The left panel holds their details. Tabs on the right show their sites, compliance documents and jobs. The compliance tab shows each document with its status approved, expiring, awaiting approval, no document uploaded, or expired. Operators can approve documents directly from here after the vendor uploads them on their side.

Jobs

A full table of jobs across all vendors with KPI cards at the top showing due today, due tomorrow, this week, overdue, flagged, and assigned to me. Operators can filter and drill into individual jobs without leaving the workcenter.

Billing

Completed jobs become transactions. Accountants can filter and select specific transactions to bundle into a bill rather than creating a single bill for everything. A bill matching flow shows the before and after impact of matching transactions to an existing bill so accountants can review the adjustment before confirming.

Compliance Templates

Templates allow operators to define a set of required compliance documents and assign them to vendors in bulk. The two-panel layout keeps the template list on the left and the document and vendor selection on the right, reducing the steps needed to set up compliance requirements across a large vendor network.

Design Approach

This workcenter was designed entirely within the AMCS Platform design system. No new patterns were introduced. Consistency was the priority operators move between multiple workcenters in a single session and the experience needs to feel like one product throughout.

Designs were validated through biweekly calls with one operator customer who reviewed progress and gave feedback on each section. The compliance template builder went through the most iteration, moving from a multi-screen flow to a single split-panel view that let operators see both their template list and the configuration at the same time.
